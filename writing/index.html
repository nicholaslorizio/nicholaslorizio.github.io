The Breach Brief

A weekly newsletter for security and business leaders, written by Nicholas P. Lorizio.

The Breach Brief publishes every Monday on Substack and LinkedIn, with every edition also on Medium. Each edition takes the week's most important security developments and explains what they mean for the people who fund, run and depend on security programs: CISOs, CIOs, CFOs, CROs and sales leaders.

The first edition was published on October 12, 2026.
