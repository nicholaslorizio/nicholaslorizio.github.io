Free and open source

Astute Nexa

A free alternative to Microsoft 365. Seven apps in one workspace on your computer, with one installer and nothing else to set up. It opens and saves .docx, .xlsx and .pptx, and your data stays on your computer.

Download for Windows Mac Linux

Version 0.1 · Free under AGPL-3.0 · See the source code

What’s inside

Write

Documents with headings, lists, tables, images, links and version history. Opens and saves .docx.

Grid

Spreadsheets with 73 built-in functions, several sheets, formatting and charts. Opens and saves .xlsx and .csv.

Deck

Slide decks with five themes, shapes and images, and a full-screen presenter. Opens and saves .pptx.

Huddle

Team channels, direct messages, threads, reactions, file sharing, and voice and video calls with screen sharing.

Relay

Mail with threads, drafts, attachments and search.

Cadence

Calendars with invites, RSVPs, free/busy, repeating events, and .ics import and export.

Vault

All your files in one place, with folders, version history, share links and Trash.

Install it on Windows

Windows 10 and 11 · 29 MB

  1. 1. Download
    Click Download free for Windows. If your browser asks, choose Keep.
  2. 2. Open the file
    Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because Nexa is new and not yet signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. 3. Next, Install, Finish
    Nexa opens in its own window and adds itself to your Start menu and desktop. No admin rights needed.

Your files stay on your computer, and uninstalling Nexa keeps them.

Install it on a Mac

macOS 11 or newer · about 60 MB. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu, About This Mac shows Chip: Apple or Processor: Intel.

Download for Apple silicon (M1 or newer) Download for Intel Macs
  1. 1. Move it to Applications
    Open the zip, then drag Astute Nexa into your Applications folder.
  2. 2. Open it once
    Double-click Astute Nexa. macOS warns that it can’t verify the app, because Nexa is new and not yet signed. Click Done.
  3. 3. Open Anyway
    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Astute Nexa, enter your Mac password and click Open. You only do this once.

On macOS 14 or older, Control-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. Nexa opens in its own window when Chrome, Edge or Brave is installed, and in Safari otherwise.

Install it on Linux

64-bit Linux

Download for Ubuntu and Debian (.deb) Download for other Linux (.tar.gz)
  1. Ubuntu, Debian, Mint, Pop!_OS
    Double-click the .deb, click Install, then open Astute Nexa from your app menu. 37 MB.
  2. Any other Linux
    Unpack the .tar.gz and run ./install.sh in the folder. It installs for your user with no password and adds Astute Nexa to your app menu. 62 MB.

Nexa opens in its own window when Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave is installed.
