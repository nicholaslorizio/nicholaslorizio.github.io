About

Nicholas P. Lorizio

Cybersecurity & AI Sales Engineer · Solutions Consultant · Author

Nicholas P. Lorizio (Nick Lorizio) is a New Hampshire–based cybersecurity and AI sales engineer, technical sales leader and author of Digitalization: The New Normal, now in its third edition: Strategies for Thriving in an AI-Driven World.

Nick has spent about ten years in technical sales for security and infrastructure. As a Technical Account Manager for Strategic Accounts at Experian, he managed more than $10 million in annual recurring revenue across data, identity and risk solutions. Before that he managed a $5 million ARR portfolio as a Technical Account Manager at Thrive, and began his career as a Sales Engineer at ACS Services.

From 2021 to 2026 he founded and led Astute Technologists, a platform and Android app that connected vetted cybersecurity and IT professionals with businesses.

His expertise spans cybersecurity architecture, cloud security on Azure, AWS, GCP and OCI, identity and access management, OT/SCADA security under NERC CIP and ISA/IEC 62443, the NIST and CIS frameworks, and AI adoption strategy.

Nick writes The Breach Brief, a weekly newsletter on Substack and LinkedIn for security and business leaders. He grew up in the Boston area and lives in New Hampshire.
