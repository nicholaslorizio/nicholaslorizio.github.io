Digitalization: The New Normal

Strategies for Thriving in an AI-Driven World · By Nicholas P. Lorizio

A fully rewritten and expanded edition for the AI era. It covers hybrid work, the incidents that reshaped cybersecurity from SolarWinds to Change Healthcare, AI as both attacker and defender, critical infrastructure and OT security, and the technology skills gap. It closes with practical security checklists for every size of organization, a 90-day plan, and a framework for explaining cyber risk to boards in terms of revenue, risk and resilience.
